Introduction

This Privacy Policy (“Privacy Policy”) explains how CredOrbit Technologies Private Limited, operating its education-fee financing platform under the brand “Schofee” (“Schofee”, “Company”, “we”, “us” or “our”), collects, uses, processes, stores, shares and protects personal data and information of users accessing or using the Schofee website, application, web application, technology platform and related services (collectively, the “Platform”).

This Privacy Policy applies to students, parents, legal guardians, applicants, borrowers, co-borrowers, educational institutions, authorised representatives of educational institutions, channel partners and other persons who interact with the Platform (“User”, “you” or “your”).

By accessing or using the Platform, or by submitting information through the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

01About Schofee

Schofee is an education-fee financing and technology platform operated by CredOrbit Technologies Private Limited.

Schofee may facilitate education-fee financing through banks, NBFCs and other regulated lending partners (“Lending Partner”).

Depending on the relevant arrangement, Schofee may act as a technology service provider and/or Lending Service Provider (“LSP”) for a Lending Partner.

Unless expressly stated otherwise, Schofee is not the lender.

Where financing is provided by a Lending Partner:

  • the Lending Partner is the actual lender;
  • the Lending Partner independently evaluates the Applicant’s creditworthiness;
  • the Lending Partner takes the final credit decision;
  • the Lending Partner determines the applicable interest, fees, tenure and other lending terms; and
  • the loan agreement, Key Facts Statement (“KFS”), sanction letter and other documents issued by the Lending Partner govern the financing facility.

02Student and Applicant/Borrower

Schofee distinguishes between the Student whose education fees are being financed and the Applicant/Borrower who applies for and, if approved, enters into the financing arrangement with the Lending Partner.

A Student may be below or above 18 years of age.

Where the Student is below 18 years of age, the Student shall not be permitted to apply for or become the borrower of an education-fee financing facility through Schofee. Schofee shall require the details of an eligible adult Applicant, who may be the Student’s parent or lawful/legal guardian, subject to the requirements of the relevant Lending Partner.

The Applicant/Borrower shall be responsible for completing applicable KYC, credit assessment, documentation, consent, e-signature, mandate and repayment requirements.

The Student’s identification on the Platform does not, by itself, create any borrowing or repayment obligation for the Student.

03Information we collect

Depending on the services used by you, we may collect the following categories of information.

A. Student Information

This may include:

  • Full name;
  • Date of birth;
  • Gender;
  • Mobile number;
  • Email ID;
  • PAN;
  • Aadhaar number;
  • Photo;
  • Student ID or admission number;
  • Educational institution;

  • Fee amount;
  • Fee invoice or fee demand;
  • Admission information;
  • Scholarship or discount information, where relevant;
  • Education-related documents; and
  • Other information reasonably necessary for the relevant service.

Where the Student is a minor, we intend to limit the collection of information relating to the Student to information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

B. Applicant/Borrower Information

Where an Applicant applies for financing, we may collect:

  • Full name;
  • Date of birth;
  • Gender;
  • PAN;
  • Aadhaar or other officially valid documents;
  • Photograph;
  • Signature;
  • Address;
  • Mobile number;
  • Email address;
  • KYC information;
  • Occupation and employment information;
  • Income information;
  • Bank account details;
  • Bank statements;
  • Existing loan and liability information;
  • Credit information;
  • Credit bureau information;
  • Tax and financial information, where applicable;
  • Loan application details;
  • Requested loan amount;
  • Tenure and financing plan;
  • Sanction and disbursement information;
  • Repayment information;
  • NACH/e-mandate information;
  • Payment and transaction details; and
  • Other information required by the Lending Partner or applicable law.

C. Parent or Legal Guardian Information

Where the Student is below 18 years of age, we may collect information relating to the parent or lawful/legal guardian who acts or proposes to act as the Applicant/Borrower.

Such information may include identity, contact, KYC, financial, banking and credit information required for processing the financing application.

The person providing information relating to a minor represents that they are authorised to provide such information and any consent required under applicable law.

D. Technical Information

When you use the Platform, we may collect certain technical information, including:

  • IP address;
  • Device information;
  • Browser type;
  • Operating system;
  • Device identifiers;
  • Login information;
  • Application activity;
  • Date and time of access;
  • Security logs; and
  • Diagnostic information.

04How we collect information

Information may be collected:

  1. Directly from you;
  2. From the Student, Applicant or parent/legal guardian;
  3. From the educational institution;
  4. From our Lending Partners;
  5. From credit information companies/bureaus;
  6. From KYC and identity-verification providers;
  7. From payment, banking and NACH service providers;
  8. Through authorised APIs and technology integrations;
  9. Through documents uploaded to the Platform;
  10. Through e-sign and electronic documentation processes;
  11. Through customer communications; and
  12. Automatically when you access or use the Platform.

05Purposes for which we use information

We may collect and process information for purposes including:

  • Creating and maintaining user accounts;
  • Identifying Students and Applicants;
  • Processing education-fee financing applications;
  • Verifying identity and conducting KYC;
  • Determining applicable eligibility requirements;
  • Facilitating credit assessment and underwriting;
  • Obtaining credit bureau information where authorised;
  • Verifying bank and financial information;
  • Verifying education and fee information;
  • Sharing relevant information with Lending Partners;
  • Preparing and executing financing documentation;
  • Facilitating e-sign and e-NACH;
  • Facilitating fee payment and disbursement;
  • Processing and tracking repayments;
  • Sending payment and EMI reminders;
  • Providing customer support;
  • Preventing fraud and misuse;
  • Maintaining security and audit trails;
  • Complying with applicable laws and regulatory requirements;
  • Handling complaints and grievances;
  • Maintaining business and transaction records;
  • Improving our Platform and services; and
  • Other purposes disclosed to you at the time of collection or otherwise permitted under applicable law.

06Processing of information relating to minors

A Student whose education fees are being financed through Schofee may be below 18 years of age.

Where information relating to a minor is processed, Schofee shall process such information in accordance with applicable data-protection laws and requirements relating to parental or lawful-guardian consent.

Where applicable law requires verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian, Schofee shall seek such consent through an appropriate process.

The parent or lawful guardian providing such consent represents that:

  • they are an adult;
  • they are the parent or lawful/legal guardian of the Student;
  • they are authorised to provide the Student’s information; and
  • the information and consent provided by them are accurate.

Schofee may undertake reasonable verification where required by applicable law.

Schofee does not permit a minor Student to become the Applicant or Borrower for an education-fee financing facility through the Platform.

07Minimisation of student data

We intend to collect only such information relating to a Student as is reasonably necessary for the relevant education-fee financing, verification, servicing, institutional and regulatory purposes.

The information required may vary depending on the financing product, Lending Partner and applicable requirements.

Schofee does not intend to collect unrelated personal information about a Student merely because the Student is identified on the Platform.

08Credit and loan processing

Where you apply for financing, information provided by you may be shared with one or more Lending Partners for evaluating and processing your application.

The Lending Partner may independently assess your creditworthiness and may approve, reject or modify the financing request in accordance with its policies and applicable law.

Submitting an application through Schofee does not guarantee approval or sanction of a loan.

The Lending Partner may obtain information from credit information companies, KYC providers, banking-data providers and other authorised sources, subject to applicable law and required consent.

Only information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose should be exchanged.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for:

Where information is no longer required, it may be deleted, anonymised or securely disposed of in accordance with applicable law and our retention policies.

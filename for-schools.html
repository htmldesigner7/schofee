Why It Matters

Large Fee Payments Shouldn’t Stand in the Way of Admissions

School fee collection can become challenging when families face difficulty managing large annual or quarterly payments. Schofee enables educational institutions to offer financing options that make fee payments more manageable for parents while helping institutions achieve faster fee realization.

Challenges Schools Face

Traditional Fee Collection Can Lead To

Disconnected, manual processes create friction for institutions and pressure for families.

01

Admission Drop-offs

Large upfront fee requirements make some families delay admission or choose another institution.

02

Delayed Fee Payments

Fees often arrive late or in parts, long after the academic term has started.

03

Unpredictable Cash Flow

Irregular collections make salaries, running costs and financial planning harder to manage.

04

Repeated Payment Follow-ups

Staff spend valuable time on reminders, calls and messages to parents every term.

05

Manual Reconciliation

Matching payments, receipts and records by hand adds workload and room for errors.

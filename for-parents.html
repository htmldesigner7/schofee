Flexible Education Financing

Education Is an Investment. Paying for It Can Be Simpler.

Education expenses can be a significant financial commitment, particularly when tuition fees are payable annually or in large instalments.

Schofee provides a digital education financing journey that helps eligible parents explore financing options and manage education expenses through applicable EMI plans.

Why Choose Schofee?

  • Secure document submission

  • Direct fee payment to the educational institution

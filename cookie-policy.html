01What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website saves on your phone, tablet or computer when you visit it. They help the website work properly, remember your choices and understand how people use it. Similar technologies, such as local storage and pixels, work in much the same way, and we call them all “cookies” in this policy.

This Cookie Policy explains how schofee by Credorbit (“Schofee”, “we”, “us”) uses cookies on our website. Please read it together with our Privacy Policy.

02Why we use cookies

  • To keep our website secure and working correctly, including forms and the application journey.
  • To remember your choices, such as your preferred settings.
  • To understand how visitors use our pages, so that we can make them faster and easier to use.
  • To measure how well our content and campaigns perform, where we choose to run them.

03Types of cookies we use

Depending on the page you visit and the choices you make, the following types of cookies may be used on our website:

TypeWhat it doesCan you switch it off?
Strictly necessaryNeeded for the website to load, stay secure and let you submit forms. Without them some parts of the site will not work.No, these are always on
FunctionalRemember your choices and settings so you do not have to repeat them.Yes
AnalyticsShow us, in aggregate, which pages are visited and how people move around, so we can improve the website.Yes
MarketingUsed only if we run advertising. They help show relevant messages and measure how well they work.Yes

We will not use cookies to collect more personal data than we need, and we do not sell your data collected through cookies.

04How long cookies stay

  • Session cookies are deleted automatically when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies stay on your device for a set period, or until you delete them, so that the website can recognise you when you return.

05Third-party cookies

Some cookies may be set by trusted third parties whose services we use, such as analytics, video, chat or social media providers. These providers have their own privacy and cookie policies, and we do not control the cookies they set. Our website may also link to other sites, such as lending partners, institutions and the Reserve Bank of India, that use their own cookies.

06How to manage or switch off cookies

You decide which cookies to allow. You can:

  • Use your browser settings to block, allow or delete cookies. Each browser is a little different, so check its “Help” section under cookies or privacy.
  • Delete cookies already saved on your device at any time.
  • Use private or incognito mode, which removes most cookies when you close the window.

If you block all cookies, including strictly necessary ones, some features, such as forms and applications, may not work properly.

07Your consent

Apart from strictly necessary cookies, we use cookies only with your consent, where the law requires it. You can withdraw or change your choice at any time using your browser settings or by contacting us. This is in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

08Cookies and your personal data

Some cookies may collect information that can identify you, such as an IP address or device details. We treat this information as personal data and handle it as set out in our Privacy Policy.

09Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when our use of cookies changes or when the law requires. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version.

Two Ways to Offer Fee Financing

Model A

No-Cost EMI

Under this model, the institution bears the applicable discounting/financing cost to provide a No-Cost EMI experience to students.

Best Suited For

Institutions focused on:

  • Improving affordability
  • Supporting admissions
  • Offering No-Cost EMI options

Benefits

  • No-Cost EMI experience for students
  • Reduced upfront payment pressure
  • Potentially improved admission conversion
  • Upfront settlement to the institution after applicable discounting
Discuss This Model
Model B

Low-Cost EMI

Under this model, the student bears the applicable financing cost while the institution receives the applicable full fee settlement without discounting.

Best Suited For

Institutions focused on:

  • Full fee realization
  • Maintaining institutional revenue
  • Offering financing flexibility to parents

Benefits

  • Full fee settlement to institution
  • No institutional discounting
  • Flexible EMI option for students
  • Reduced fee collection dependency

01About Schofee

Schofee is an education-fee financing and technology platform operated by CredOrbit Technologies Private Limited.

Schofee may act as a technology platform and/or Lending Service Provider (“LSP”) for banks, NBFCs or other regulated lending partners (“Lending Partner”).

Unless expressly stated otherwise, Schofee is not the lender.

Where financing is provided by a Lending Partner:

  • the Lending Partner is the actual lender;
  • the Lending Partner independently evaluates the Applicant’s creditworthiness;
  • the Lending Partner takes the final credit decision;
  • the Lending Partner determines the applicable interest, fees, tenure and other lending terms; and
  • the financing facility is governed by the Lending Partner’s KFS, sanction letter, loan agreement, repayment schedule and other applicable documents.

02Definitions

For these Terms:

  • “Student” means the person whose education fees are being paid or financed through Schofee.
  • “Applicant” means the eligible person who submits an application for education-fee financing through Schofee.
  • “Borrower” means the person who enters into the applicable loan or financing agreement with the Lending Partner.
  • “Parent/Legal Guardian” means the parent or lawful/legal guardian of a Student, as applicable.
  • “Institute” means the educational institution whose fees are being financed or paid.
  • “Lending Partner” means the bank, NBFC or other regulated lending entity providing the financing facility.

03Student and Applicant/Borrower

Schofee distinguishes between the Student whose education fees are being financed and the Applicant/Borrower who applies for and, if approved, enters into the financing arrangement.

A Student may be below or above 18 years of age.

Where the Student is below 18 years of age, the Student shall not be permitted to apply for or become the Applicant or Borrower for an education-fee financing facility through Schofee.

In such circumstances, Schofee shall require the details of an eligible adult Applicant, who may be the Student’s parent or lawful/legal guardian, subject to the Lending Partner’s eligibility requirements.

The Applicant/Borrower shall be responsible for completing applicable KYC, credit assessment, documentation, consent, e-signature, mandate and repayment requirements.

The Student’s identification on the Platform does not by itself create any borrowing or repayment obligation for the Student.

04Age and information declaration

Users shall provide accurate information regarding the Student’s age and identity.

If the Student is below 18 years of age, the Platform may require the Applicant to provide additional information concerning their identity and relationship with the Student.

The Applicant represents that all information provided is true, complete and accurate.

Schofee may reject, suspend or terminate an application where it reasonably identifies false, misleading or fraudulent information.

05Parent/Legal Guardian

Where the Student is below 18 years of age, the parent or lawful/legal guardian applying for financing represents that:

  • they are an adult;
  • they are the Student’s parent or lawful/legal guardian;
  • they are authorised to provide information relating to the Student;
  • the information provided is accurate;
  • they are authorised to provide any consent required under applicable law; and
  • if approved as the Borrower, they understand that the financing and repayment obligations are theirs and not those of the minor Student.

Schofee and/or the Lending Partner may undertake reasonable verification of such information.
